The note in the top corner explains the entire machine in one breath. The
engine turns the outer rim; the blades set into the rim make lift; the lift
carries the city; and the rollers, plus the inertia of the main structure,
keep the whole thing from tipping over.

I have since learned several good reasons this does not fly. I have not yet
made a drawing I believe in as completely.
