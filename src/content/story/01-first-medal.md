Gold, and a ribbon long enough to reach my knees. I remember the weight of it
better than I remember the bout.

Behind me the next fight had already started. Nobody warns you that the hall
does not stop for your turn — it just moves on to the next square of mat, and
you are left holding the thing you came for.
